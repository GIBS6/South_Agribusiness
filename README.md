## 1. Executive Summary

This repository contains the end-to-end industry taxonomy, structural economic census, regulatory audit trail, and commercial validation architecture for South African agribusiness.

Rather than building unverified software, this project enforces an evidence-grounded research protocol to identify systemic friction across South African agricultural inputs (fertilizers, pesticides, stock remedies, and agricultural remedies under Act 36 of 1947 and SANS 10234:2019/GHS).

### Primary Commercial Vehicle
* **Product:** Agricultural Product Compliance Forensic Audit — 5 Change Scenarios (`COM-001`)
* **Positioning:** Compliance Operations Control Layer (Change-Management & Evidence Architecture)
* **Pricing Hypothesis:** R7,500 + VAT (Fixed-Scope Technical Diagnostic)
* **Target Cohort:** Generic formulators, diversified agricultural remedy distributors, toll manufacturers, and compliance consultancies (`CUS-004`).

## 3. Governance & Research Protocol

Every empirical assertion and market datum in this repository is governed by the **Claims & Evidence Protocol** (`CLAIMS_REGISTER.md`):

1. `[FACT]`: Formally verified against official gazettes, statutory acts, company registrations, or primary audit documents.
2. `[CALCULATED]`: Mathematically derived model based on explicit, stated baseline assumptions.
3. `[UNVERIFIED]`: Market intelligence, industry estimates, or operational claims awaiting primary field verification.
4. `[HYPOTHESIS]`: Commercial, pricing, or product assumptions undergoing active field falsification.
