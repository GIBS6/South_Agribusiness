# REGULATORY BASELINE

Initial regulatory anchor:
Fertilizers, Farm Feeds, Agricultural Remedies and Stock Remedies Act, 1947
(Act 36 of 1947).

Initial questions to verify for every product/service:
- Is the activity regulated?
- Is the product regulated?
- Who is the regulator?
- Does the business need registration?
- Does the product need registration?
- Does the facility need registration/licensing?
- Are labels prescribed?
- Are containers prescribed?
- Are SDS requirements applicable?
- Are laboratory tests required?
- Are efficacy/residue/toxicology studies required?
- Are import requirements applicable?
- Are environmental obligations applicable?
- Are occupational health and safety obligations applicable?
- What records must be retained?
- What are renewal cycles and fees?

Never answer these from memory when a current authoritative source exists.
