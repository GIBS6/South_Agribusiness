### 1.2 Customer Stratification & Priority Tiers

#### 🎯 TIER 1 (PRIMARY): Small/Medium Generic Remedy Formulators
- **Profile:** 10–60 registered commercial SKUs; domestic chemical formulation plant; 15–80 employees; lean technical team (1–2 regulatory/technical officers).
- **Current System:** MS Word templates, Excel registration matrices, shared PDF network drives, email attachments.
- **Why They Buy:** Constantly reformulating due to active ingredient sourcing shifts (China/India imports); high label revision velocity; customers and co-ops constantly demanding updated GHS SDSs; extreme anxiety over DoEL factory audits and Act 36 label mismatches.
- **The Core Value Proposition:**
  > *"Give me one controlled place where I can know what products we have, what documents exist, which version is current, what is missing, and what needs immediate attention."*

#### 🎯 TIER 2 (PRIMARY): Generic Registrants & Agricultural Brand Owners
- **Profile:** Hold 10–40 Act 36 L-Number registrations; completely asset-light; manufacture 100% via third-party toll formulators; distribute through independent retail agronomists and co-ops.
- **Operating Model:**
  $$\text{Registrant / Brand Owner} \longrightarrow \text{Toll Blender} \longrightarrow \text{Distributor} \longrightarrow \text{Farmer}$$
- **Current System:** Disconnected Dropbox/Google Drive folders, consultant email threads, outdated batch master copies.
- **Why They Buy:** **The Coordination Friction.** They do not touch physical chemicals, but legally carry 100% statutory liability for every label and SDS. They need an immutable evidence vault that links registration dossiers, raw material supplier certificates, and packaging artwork.

#### 🎯 TIER 3 (PRIMARY): Contract Chemical Toll Blenders
- **Profile:** High-throughput liquid and powder chemical blending facilities (Spartan, Wadeville, Durban, Paarl); blending products on behalf of 5 to 15 different label-holder clients.
- **Why They Buy:** Massive document turnover. They handle raw chemical technical imports arriving with Chinese GB-standard SDSs and must maintain compliant SANS 10234 shop-floor documentation for workers under the Occupational Health and Safety Act (OHSA HCA Regs 2021). Missing or incorrect SDSs halt production lines.

#### 🎯 TIER 4 (SECONDARY): Specialty Fertiliser & Biostimulant Blenders
- **Profile:** 5–30 products; 5–20 employees; fast-growing sales in foliar feeds, micro-element chelates, and microbial soil enhancers registered under Act 36 GN R. 972 (Group 2 and Group 3).
- **Why They Buy:** High commercial approachability (founder/MD accessible directly); zero full-time regulatory personnel (founder or lead chemist does paperwork at night); increasing pressure from commercial farmers and export packhouses for 16-point GHS-compliant SDSs.

#### 🎯 TIER 5 (SECONDARY): Agricultural Remedy Importers & Private-Label Distributors
- **Profile:** Regional input distribution businesses (10–30 retail branches) that import generic formulated remedies or pack under their own private label.
- **Why They Buy:** Co-op retail counter compliance; must supply instant, searchable SDSs to hundreds of commercial farmers to satisfy export GlobalG.A.P. audits.

#### 🤝 TIER 6 (CHANNEL / MULTIPLIER): Independent Regulatory Consultants
- **Profile:** Boutique regulatory practices (1–4 consultants) managing Act 36 dossier submissions and SDS authoring for 10 to 30 agricultural input companies on retainers or per-document fees.
- **Why They Partner / Buy:** They are not competitors; they are our **highest-leverage distribution channel**.
  $$\text{1 Consultant Account} \Longrightarrow \text{15 to 25 Underlying Formulator Client Portfolios}$$
  By adopting our workspace, the consultant cuts document authoring time from 8 hours to 30 minutes, eliminates calculation errors, and manages all their client dossiers in one secure multi-tenant environment.
