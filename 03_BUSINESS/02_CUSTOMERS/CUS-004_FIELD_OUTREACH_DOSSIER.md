### 5.2 Minutes 03:00–12:00 — The Operational Change-Chain Discovery

```
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                    THE CHANGE-CHAIN DISCOVERY                                      |
|  Trigger -> People -> Documents -> Calculations -> Approvals -> Systems -> Handoffs -> Evidence   |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

    *   *Verbatim:* *"When an active ingredient purity shifts or a surfactant is substituted, how do you currently identify every single downstream document, calculation, label proof, and approval affected by that change?"*

    *   *Target Signal:* Measures handoff latency and communication silos.
3.  **Question 3 (The Tooling & Calculation Architecture):**
    *   *Verbatim:* *"What systems coordinate that change? Is it managed through your ERP (Syspro/SAP), shared network folders, email threads, or MS Word/Excel files?"*
    *   *Target Signal:* Validates whether incumbent software solves dependency tracking or if disconnected files dominate.
4.  **Question 4 (Latency & Cycle Time):**
    *   *Verbatim:* *"From the day a supplier specification changes until updated packaging labels, audit-ready SDSs, and registration notifications are fully finalized and released, how many calendar weeks or months does that cycle consume?"*
    *   *Target Signal:* Measures real business latency and operational drag.
5.  **Question 5 (External Spend / Quantified Budget):**
    *   *Verbatim:* *"Do you use outside consultancies or toxicologists to re-evaluate classifications or re-draft files when changes occur? What does that coordination cost the business annually?"*
