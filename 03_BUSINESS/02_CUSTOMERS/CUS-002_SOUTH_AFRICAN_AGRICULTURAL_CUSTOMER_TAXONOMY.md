In early-stage venture development, the most dangerous cognitive bias is **product-induced myopia**—categorizing an entire multi-hundred-billion rand industry through the lens of a single proposed software feature.

[`CUS-001`](file:///C:/Projects/Personal/South_Agribusiness/03_BUSINESS/02_CUSTOMERS/CUSTOMER_UNIVERSE_REGISTER.md) mapped 30 target companies specifically for **Candidate 1 (Agricultural Product Compliance Management)**. - **Entity:** **Villa Crop Protection (Pty) Ltd** (Kempton Park, Gauteng) `[FACT]`
- **Sector Lineage:**
  $$\text{02. Inputs} \longrightarrow \text{Crop Protection} \longrightarrow \text{Generic Formulator / Brand Owner} \longrightarrow \text{Upstream}$$

### Deep Dive 04: The Perishable Export Quality Inspectorate
- **Entity:** **PPECB (Perishable Products Export Control Board)** (Cape Town / National) `[FACT]`
- **Sector Lineage:**
  $$\text{12. Quality, Testing & Certification} \longrightarrow \text{Export Horticulture & Perishables} \longrightarrow \text{Statutory Quality Body} \longrightarrow \text{Verification}$$
- **Multi-Dimensional Profile:**
  - *Commodity Served:* All exported South African citrus, deciduous fruit, subtropicals, table grapes, vegetables, meat, and dairy.
  - *Business Model:* Statutory mandate under Act 9 of 1983; self-funding through statutory inspection and cold-chain verification levies charged per pallet/carton.
