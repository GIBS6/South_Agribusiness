# SOUTH AFRICA AGRIBUSINESS — PROJECT CHARTER

Status: DISCOVERY
Owner: Founder / CEO
Technical builder: Antigravity
Architecture/specification/QA: ChatGPT
Geography: South Africa
Principle: Evidence before implementation.

## Objective

Design a real, legally compliant, commercially viable South African agribusiness.
The final business may operate in one or more layers of the agricultural value chain:
inputs, services, primary production, aggregation, storage, processing, distribution,
technology, or combinations thereof.

## Non-negotiables

1. No invented market data.
2. No invented regulatory requirements.
3. Every material claim gets a source.
4. Regulatory status is verified against authoritative South African sources.
5. Standards are recorded by exact designation, title, edition/status and applicability.
6. Product registration is never assumed merely because a product is commercially available.
7. Manufacturing, importing, distributing and selling are treated as distinct compliance questions.
8. Software is built only after business rules are approved.
9. Antigravity receives implementation packets, not open-ended instructions.
10. Every release must pass QA and compliance gates.

## Core research question

Where in the South African agricultural value chain can we build a profitable,
defensible business with realistic capital requirements and recurring revenue?

## Candidate domains

- Fertiliser and plant nutrition
- Agricultural remedies: herbicides, fungicides, insecticides and related categories
- Seeds and planting material
- Soil and agricultural laboratory services
- Agronomy and farm-support services
- Mechanisation and spraying services
- Irrigation
- Livestock feed
- Agro-processing
- Storage and cold chain
- Agricultural logistics
- Agricultural technology
- Procurement and distribution
- Data, traceability and compliance systems

## Decision rule

No business model becomes ACTIVE until:
Research -> Evidence -> Regulatory assessment -> Unit economics -> Risk review
-> Founder approval -> Implementation specification.
