# RESEARCH CONTROL

Research is divided into:
1. Industry structure
2. Market and demand
3. Commodity economics
4. Agricultural inputs
5. Regulation
6. Business models
7. Competition
8. Operations
9. Technology

Source hierarchy:
A. South African legislation / regulations / government notices
B. Official department and regulator publications
C. SABS / accredited standards information
D. Statistics South Africa
E. Industry bodies and recognised research institutions
F. Audited company disclosures
G. Reputable market research
H. Secondary commentary

Claims must be classified:
FACT / ESTIMATE / ASSUMPTION / HYPOTHESIS / UNKNOWN

UNKNOWN is preferable to unsupported certainty.
