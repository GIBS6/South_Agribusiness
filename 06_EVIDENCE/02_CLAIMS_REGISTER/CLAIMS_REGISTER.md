- `FACT`: Verified by statutory text or official government/industry census data.
- `ESTIMATE`: Statistical estimate from recognized bodies (DALRRD, Stats SA, Agbiz).
- `ASSUMPTION`: Commercial or operational deductions based on current structure.
- `HYPOTHESIS`: Strategic business propositions awaiting empirical testing.
- `UNKNOWN`: Identified knowledge gaps requiring primary investigation.
