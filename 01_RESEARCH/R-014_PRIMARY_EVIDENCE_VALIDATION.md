## 1. THE NON-NEGOTIABLE VALIDATION CHARTER

### 1.1 The Scientific Rule of Disconfirmation
In `R-012` and `R-013`, **Opportunity 01 (Agricultural Product Compliance Management)** survived a 20-contender tournament and an independent forensic deep-dive. However, internal research can create dangerous confirmation bias:
> **A high score on an internal analytical matrix is merely a well-reasoned hypothesis. It is NOT customer proof. Customers do not care about our scoring models, our 19 sectors, or our strategic flywheels. Customers only care about their own operational agony, their regulatory fears, and their personal budgets.**

`R-014` enforces a strict scientific boundary:
1. **Never use our own R-012/R-013 assumptions as evidence during customer discovery.**  
   The customer must describe their reality unprompted. We do not lead the witness, we do not pitch "our platform," and we do not quote our own market sizing numbers.
2. **A failed hypothesis is a successful experiment.**  
   If customer discovery proves that formulators refuse to pay, that consultants are entrenched and adequate, or that regulatory liability is uninsurable, **we kill Candidate 1 immediately without regret**. Killing an unviable candidate at the discovery stage saves hundreds of thousands of rands in wasted software development.
3. **Only financial or costly commitments count as proof.**  
   Polite enthusiasm ("That sounds very interesting, let me know when it launches") is recorded as **ZERO EVIDENCE**. Only commitments of real data (unredacted SDS/label files) and real money (paid audits, pilot deposits) constitute validation.

## 2. THE SIX VALIDATION GATES (THE INTERROGATION ENGINE)

---

### GATE 1: PROBLEM EXISTENCE (WORKFLOW REALITY)
*Objective: Determine whether the manual documentation bottleneck actually occurs on the ground, who executes it, and its true frequency.*

```
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                    GATE 1: AUDIT CHECKLIST                                         |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```
- [ ] **1.1 Workflow Actor:** Who physically authors, updates, and formats Safety Data Sheets (SDSs) and product label text? Is it an in-house formulation chemist, an internal regulatory officer, or an outsourced consultant?
- [ ] **1.2 Portfolio Volume:** How many active commercial SKUs (remedies, foliar feeds, biostimulants, adjuvants) does the company actively market in South Africa?
- [ ] **1.3 Revision Velocity:** How many SDS or label revisions were physically produced over the preceding 12 months? What triggered each revision? (e.g., active ingredient supplier change, GN 3812 GHS mandate, raw material impurity shift, customer audit query).
- [ ] **1.4 The Rework Hotspot:** Where does work get stuck? In calculating SANS 10234 acute toxicity estimates (ATE)? In matching mandatory GHS pictograms and hazard phrases? In formatting the 16 standard SANS sections? Or in waiting for internal technical signatory review?

---

### GATE 2: PAIN SEVERITY (THE ECONOMIC & LEGAL DAMAGE)
*Objective: Quantify the exact cost, delay, and consequence when compliance documentation fails or stalls.*

```
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                    GATE 2: AUDIT CHECKLIST                                         |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```
- [ ] **2.1 Registration Delays:** In the last 24 months, has an Act 36 registration dossier, label amendment, or renewal been queried, delayed, or rejected by the Registrar at DALRRD due to documentation, formatting, or classification errors?
  - *Quantification:* How many calendar months of delay did the query letter (*"dely-brief"*) add to the commercial launch?
  - *Lost Sales:* What was the estimated gross margin lost per month while that product was barred from the commercial market?
- [ ] **2.2 Audit Non-Conformances & Quarantines:** Has a retail distributor (e.g., Kaap Agri, Senwes, Laeveld Agrochem) or a commercial farming client ever quarantined stock, rejected delivery, or refused payment due to an outdated or non-GHS-compliant SDS?
- [ ] **2.3 Factory & Workplace Safety Audits:** Has the Department of Employment and Labour (DoEL) conducted an unannounced workplace hazardous chemical inspection at the blending plant under the Hazardous Chemical Agents Regulations 2021? What non-conformances were issued?
- [ ] **2.4 Consultant Billing Inefficiency:** What did the company pay in total over the last financial year to external regulatory and chemical safety consultants? How long did external consultants take to return completed documents (days vs. weeks)?

---

### GATE 3: EXISTING SOLUTIONS (WHY HASN'T IT BEEN AUTOMATED?)
*Objective: Uncover why the company continues to tolerate manual Word/Excel processes and evaluate incumbent software moats.*

```
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                    GATE 3: AUDIT CHECKLIST                                         |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```
- [ ] **3.1 Current Tooling:** What software does the team currently touch on a daily basis? (e.g., MS Word templates, Excel calculation macros, shared Google Drive/Dropbox folders, generic ERP like Syspro/SAP, or CropLife Agri-Intel).
- [ ] **3.2 The Agri-Intel Boundary:** How exactly does the company use CropLife SA’s Agri-Intel database?
  - *Verification:* Do they use it strictly as a read-only lookup for registered MRLs and competitor labels, or does it assist in *authoring* their internal SDSs and calculating their mixture hazard profiles?
- [ ] **3.3 International Chemical Software Evaluation:** Has the company ever evaluated global chemical authoring software (e.g., Sphera, Chemwatch, UL WERCS, Lisam)?
  - *Failure Modes:* Why did they reject or abandon them? (e.g., Prohibitive USD pricing >$25k/yr, lack of South African Act 36 label formatting templates, inability to handle local emergency response numbers).
- [ ] **3.4 The In-House Automation Inertia:** Why hasn't their internal IT team or lead chemist built an internal automated database? (e.g., Lack of dedicated software developers, constant regulatory changes, technical debt).

### GATE 4: ECONOMIC BUYER (IDENTIFYING THE CHECKBOOK)
*Objective: Identify the exact individual with the organizational authority to approve a commercial subscription.*

```
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                    GATE 4: AUDIT CHECKLIST                                         |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```
- [ ] **4.1 Purchasing Authority:** If an automated compliance engine was proven to cut document authoring time by 80% and guarantee 100% SANS 10234 mathematical accuracy, who in the company has the formal authority to sign an agreement?
  - *Title:* Managing Director / CEO? Technical Director? Financial Director / CFO? Or Head of SHEQ?
- [ ] **4.2 Budget Allocation Line:** Where does compliance software spend come from?
  - *Budget Source:* Quality / SHEQ operational overhead? R&D and Registration budget? Or General Administrative expenses?
- [ ] **4.3 Procurement Friction:** Does an expenditure of R7,500 once-off or R4,500/month require board approval, a formal corporate tender process, or can it be authorized directly on a company credit card / departmental sign-off?
- [ ] **4.4 Internal Resistance & Objections:** Who inside the company would fight against adopting a new compliance system? (e.g., An entrenched regulatory officer who fears software makes their job redundant; an IT manager who demands on-premise installation).

### GATE 6: REGULATORY & LIABILITY REALITY (THE LEGAL PERIMETER)
*Objective: Verify that the legal boundary between deterministic software calculation and statutory human sign-off holds firm in practice.*

```
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                    GATE 6: AUDIT CHECKLIST                                         |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```
