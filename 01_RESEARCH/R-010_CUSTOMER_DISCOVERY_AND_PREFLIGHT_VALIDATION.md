## 3. WORKSTREAM 2: CUSTOMER DISCOVERY PROTOCOL & INTERVIEW ENGINE

### 3.1 The "Mom Test" Agribusiness Discovery Methodology
Customer discovery in the South African agricultural input sector fails if the founder asks hypothetical questions ("Would you buy software that automatically creates GHS labels?"). Agribusiness managers will politely say "yes" to be supportive, giving false validation.

The discovery protocol strictly enforces **The Mom Test** rules:
1. **Talk about their past actions and current reality, not hypothetical futures.**
2. **Ask about specific instances of cost, rework, rejections, and delays.**
3. **Never pitch the product, show mockups, or mention "SaaS" until the interview questions are fully answered.**
4. **Identify who actually signs the check versus who actually does the typing.**

### 3.2 The 25 Forensic Interview Questions

```
========================================================================================
                      THE 25-QUESTION CUSTOMER DISCOVERY ENGINE
========================================================================================
```

#### MODULE A: Current Workflow, Tools & Velocity (Understanding the Baseline)
- **Q01:** How many active commercial SKUs (remedies, adjuvants, foliar feeds, fertilisers) does your company currently sell in South Africa?
- **Q02:** Walk me through the exact step-by-step process of what happens when a formulation is updated, an active ingredient supplier changes, or a new product is launched. Who touches the document first?

- **Q05:** Where are your master regulatory files stored, and how do you ensure that the label being printed on the packaging line in the factory matches the exact version approved by the Registrar?

#### MODULE B: Rework, Audit Friction & Registrar Bottlenecks (Quantifying Pain)
- **Q06:** In the last two years, have you had an application or label amendment queried, delayed, or rejected by the Registrar of Act 36 at DALRRD? What was the specific cause?
- **Q07:** When an Act 36 submission is queried by the Department due to labelling, formatting, or classification errors, how much calendar time does that rework add to your product launch?
- **Q08:** Following the mandatory implementation of the Regulations for Hazardous Chemical Agents (2021) and GN 3812 in August 2023, what percentage of your existing commercial portfolio has been fully converted to SANS 10234 GHS compliance?
- **Q09:** What is the single most frustrating or time-consuming part of calculating the physical, health, and environmental hazard classifications for a complex mixture with multiple inerts and surfactants?
- **Q10:** Have you ever experienced a packaging recall, retail distributor quarantine, or stock quarantine at a co-op because of an incorrect label, outdated SDS, or missing GHS hazard pictogram? What did that cost the business?

#### MODULE C: Signatory Accountability & Liability Allocation (Identifying the Responsible Actor)
- **Q11:** In your company, who is the designated statutory "Responsible Person" or Technical Signatory who legally signs off on the Act 36 registration dossier and the SANS 10234 classification?
- **Q12:** If the Department of Employment and Labour (DoEL) conducts an unannounced workplace inspection at one of your retail dealers or toll-formulation sites and finds a non-compliant SDS, whose neck is on the line legally?
- **Q13:** How do you verify that your raw material suppliers (especially technical active imports from China or India) are providing legitimate, SANS 10234-compliant SDSs rather than generic Chinese GB-standard documents?
- **Q14:** Do you employ full-time in-house regulatory affairs personnel, or do you rely on external consultants? If in-house, how many people, and what percentage of their weekly time is spent purely on document formatting and data entry?

#### MODULE D: External Consultant Spend & True Cost Structure (Uncovering Latent Spend)
- **Q15:** When you use external regulatory or chemical consultants (e.g., Riskchem, independent toxicologists, regulatory agents) to author an SDS or review a label, what do they charge per document?
- **Q16:** How long does an external consultant typically take to return a completed, compliant 16-point SDS to you?
- **Q17:** What did your company spend in total over the last financial year on external regulatory consulting, dossier compilation, and chemical compliance auditing?
- **Q18:** How do you currently use CropLife SA's `Agri-Intel` database? Does it assist you in *generating* and *auditing* your internal compliance, or is it strictly a lookup repository?

#### MODULE E: Purchase Authority, Budget & Commercial Triggers (Testing the Transaction)
- **Q19:** If a solution could guarantee 100% SANS 10234 mathematical classification accuracy and generate a fully compliant 16-point SDS and Act 36 label layout in under 15 minutes, who in your organisation would have the formal authority to approve an ongoing subscription for it?
- **Q20:** What internal hurdle or procurement process must a new software or compliance tool pass in your company? (e.g., Managing Director sign-off, IT security audit, board approval?)
- **Q21:** Does regulatory compliance come out of the R&D/Technical budget, the Quality/SHEQ budget, or General Operational overheads?
- **Q22:** What would be the biggest internal objection if you presented a proposal to adopt an automated chemical compliance management platform? (e.g., "We don't trust software with formulation recipes", "We already pay a consultant", "We don't have budget")
- **Q23:** What specific evidence or proof would you need to see before you would allow your technical team to use such a system?
- **Q24:** Would you be willing to share an anonymised, non-confidential formulation sheet or an existing problematic SDS with us so we can run a free, blind SANS 10234 compliance gap audit for you?
- **Q25:** If that blind audit uncovered material errors or non-compliance risks in your active commercial documents, what would you do next?
