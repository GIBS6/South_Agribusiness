### 1.2 Information Boundaries: The "State Machine" of Agriculture
The master insight established in `CUS-003` is that modern agriculture is fundamentally a **sequential state machine with high-friction information boundaries at almost every handoff**:

```
[Input Mfr] ──(1)──> [Registrant] ──(2)──> [Distributor] ──(3)──> [Agro-Dealer] ──(4)──> [Farmer]
                                                                                              │
[Retailer] <──(11)── [Exporter] <──(10)── [Processor] <──(9)── [Lab] <──(8)── [Silo] <──(7)── [Transport] <──(6)── [Harvest] (5)
```

At every numbered boundary $(1) \rightarrow (11)$, value, money, goods, and legal responsibility change hands:
$$\textbf{Information} \longrightarrow \textbf{Provenance} \longrightarrow \textbf{Transformation} \longrightarrow \textbf{State} \longrightarrow \textbf{Decision}$$

Whenever the information layer fails, deadweight loss occurs (fines, rejected export containers, empty trucks, disputed grain grades, or unpaid supplier invoices). **The business that controls the reconciliation and verification layer at these boundaries captures the margin.**
